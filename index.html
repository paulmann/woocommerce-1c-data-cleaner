woocommerce-1c-data-cleaner

Break the old 1C link, keep every product

Single-file PHP 8.3 tool that resets WooCommerce to 1C exchange metadata for the ItGalaxy plugin: clears stale GUIDs, price and stock links without deleting a single product.

What it does

  • Clears stale GUIDs, price and stock bindings
  • Never touches posts or product rows
  • Password-protected UI with IP binding
